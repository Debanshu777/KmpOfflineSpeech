Eigen source availability

The pinned sherpa-onnx 2.0.1 source configuration references Eigen 5.0.1. Eigen is primarily licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0; retain its accompanying license and copyright notices.

The unmodified Eigen 5.0.1 Source Code Form is publicly available at:
https://gitlab.com/libeigen/eigen/-/archive/5.0.1/eigen-5.0.1.tar.gz
SHA-256: e9c326dc8c05cd1e044c71f30f1b2e34a6161a3b6ecf445d56b53ff1669e3dec

Download the archive above and verify that SHA-256 before extracting it. The archive includes the source files and their MPL 2.0 and other applicable license notices. The included COPYING.README describes the licensing; COPYING.MPL2 contains the MPL 2.0 text. Siren also retains Eigen's LICENSE and COPYING.* files under META-INF/siren/licenses/eigen/.

The inspected sherpa-onnx 2.0.1 cmake/eigen.cmake downloads this exact archive without a source-patching step. Siren copies the upstream runtime binary bytes without modifying them. This statement supplies a source location; it does not change the incomplete status of the separate native-binary redistribution audit.
